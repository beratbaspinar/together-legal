6. Live Location Privacy

Live Location is optional. It begins only after you choose to enable the feature and grant the necessary operating-system location permission.

When Live Location is active, your current precise location may be shared with your connected partner.

If you separately enable background sharing, Together may continue receiving location updates while the App is in the background, subject to iOS permissions, operating-system behaviour, battery-saving behaviour, and device state.

When location permission is disabled

If Together detects that location updates cannot continue because permission or Location Services have been disabled, the latest Live Location record may remain stored but will be marked as not actively shared.

While suspended:

  • your partner cannot access the stored suspended location through Together;
  • the stored location is not treated as a current Live Location; and
  • a fresh location fix is required before sharing becomes active again.

When you choose Stop sharing

If you explicitly choose Stop sharing, Together deletes your current Live Location row.

Force-quitting the App

Force-quitting Together stops the app process and can stop new location updates. A previously stored location may remain until sharing is suspended, explicitly stopped, you leave the couple, or your account is deleted.

Live Location should not be relied upon for emergencies or personal safety.

11. Account Deletion

You can initiate account deletion from within Together.

If your connected partner remains, shared couple content such as memories, goals, photos, dates, and places may remain available to that partner.

If you are the last member of the couple, the couple space is deleted according to the Service’s deletion process.

Your profile, account, personal Live Location, personal snaps, and other user-specific records are removed according to the deletion process.

App Store subscriptions are separate. Deleting your Together account does not automatically cancel an active Apple App Store subscription. You must manage or cancel the subscription separately through Apple if you do not want future renewals.

Apple and RevenueCat may retain transaction or customer records independently according to their own legal obligations and policies.
