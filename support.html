Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions about Together.

How do I connect with my partner?

Together uses an invitation flow to connect two accounts to a shared couple space. Follow the instructions in the app to share or enter the invitation information.

Can both of us use Together Premium?

Yes. When one person subscribes to Together Premium, both connected partners can enjoy Premium benefits in their shared couple space.

How can I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions purchased through Apple are managed through your Apple account. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions to review or cancel an active subscription. Deleting your Together account does not automatically cancel your Apple subscription.

How does Live Location work?

Live Location is optional. You must choose to enable it and grant the required iOS location permissions. When sharing is active, your current location may be visible to your connected partner. You can stop sharing from within the app.

How do I delete my account?

Use the account-deletion option inside Together. If you cannot access your account, email us for help. Some content in a shared couple space may remain accessible to your partner after you delete your account; see our Privacy Notice for details.

What if I have a problem signing in or using a feature?

Make sure you have an internet connection and are using a current version of Together. If the issue continues, contact us at support.together06@gmail.com with a short description of what you see.
